# Amendment 39 — Config G headline cells re-run under the settled metric + 0.30 m goal band, raw trajectories archived

**Status:** frozen before any run. No amend39 number exists. Registered before the code ran on any GPU.

**How it serves the goal (rule 1):** the paper's headline scaling results (N crossover, safe_N, compensation gap,
delay-removal mechanism, pure-staleness sweep, margin choice, gate sweep, layer split across N) are currently scored at
the final instant T. That metric over-counts arrival (review_amend36/37/38), and the raw trajectories are lost, so
those claims cannot be stated under the final metric without re-running the cells.

**One question (rule 4):** do the Config G headline results hold when arrival is scored as *settled* (the amend37
definition) and with a goal tolerance band of one drone body (0.30 m)?

**What is NOT done:** no new arm, no new geometry, no new predictor, no parameter change, no new threshold. Every cell is
a byte-for-byte copy of a registered cell except `run_name`. The band is the only new metric (see rule 5 note).

## Metrics (computed per rollout on the pod, from the full trajectory, immediately after the rollout)

Notation: pos(t) true positions, every step (dt 0.01 s) plus pos_final; g goal centre; r_goal goal radius;
collided_i = agent i's true distance to any agent < D_COLLISION = 0.30 m at any step (amend12 agent_facts, unchanged).

- `inside_b(t)_i` = ‖pos_i(t) − g‖ ≤ r_goal + b.
- `settled_{W,b}_i` = inside_b over the last W s (the last round(W/dt)+1 samples, pos_final included) has mean ≥ 0.90.
- `completeness_settled_{W,b}` = mean_i settled; `safe_settled_{W,b}` = mean_i (settled ∧ ¬collided).
- W ∈ {3, 5, 10} s; **primary W = 5 s**. b ∈ {0, 0.30} m; **primary b = 0** (the amend37 metric), **secondary
  b = 0.30**.
- Also recorded: completeness@T, safe@T and ever_collided (recomputed; must equal the ladder's own metrics_seed json
  on collision_pair_steps and completeness_final, otherwise the rollout is flagged).
- With b = 0 this is exactly the amend37/38 implementation (experiments/amend38_settled_recompute.arm_rows).

**Rule-5 note.** The 0.30 m band is a metric introduced after results were seen. It was requested explicitly
(2026-11-05) on the basis of review_amend38 B, where the non-settlers sit within about one drone body of the rim. It is
reported *alongside* the primary b = 0 metric and never replaces it. No claim is allowed to rest on b = 0.30 alone.

## Registered analyses and readings (seed-level bootstrap 10k, 95 %)

- **Q1 safe_N (primary, W=5, b=0):** the amend22 definition with completeness replaced by completeness_settled:
  the largest N such that at it and every smaller N, the ever_collided CI upper bound is ≤ 0.10 **and** mean
  completeness_settled ≥ 0.90. Reported for real 0.55, real 0.70, learned 0.70 and noise_only 0.70, for b = 0 and
  b = 0.30. Reading: learned safe_N = 50 is **confirmed** if it is reached at b = 0; **refuted** if it is lower at b = 0.
- **Q2 compensation gap:** learned 0.70 − real 0.70 safe_settled per N (paired by seed). It holds at N if the CI
  excludes 0.
- **Q3 mechanism:** noise_only ever_collided per N (metric-independent; reproduction only) and noise_only
  safe_settled per N (new: is the delay-free swarm also *settled-complete* in Config G?). No threshold. Reported as is.
- **Q4 pure staleness:** learned and real safe_settled per T_frame, learned − real (CI). Decomposition as amend26 Q3:
  learned ever at fixed N=100 vs the crossover at equal T_frame (descriptive), plus learned completeness_settled at
  fixed N vs the crossover (descriptive). The amend26 reading "completeness tracks density" holds if
  completeness_settled at fixed N=100 stays ≥ 0.90 at every T_frame while it falls below 0.90 at N ≥ 150.
- **Q5 margin rule (amend23 rule, unchanged except the metric):** among margins with mean completeness_settled ≥ 0.90,
  pick the best mean safe_settled. If 0.70 is no longer selected at N=100, this is reported as a change and the paper
  is updated. The operating margin of the other stages is **not** changed within this amendment.
- **Q6 gate:** learned − real safe_settled per gate (CI); learned ever per gate (reproduction).
- **Q7 layer split (stage D):** MFG-only vs none, CBF-only vs both, per N, under safe_settled (CI). The amend27 readings
  are applied unchanged.

## Raw-trajectory archive (the amend21–27 lesson)

- After each rollout: `traj_seed{s}.npz` = pos (float32, every step), pos_final, collided (bool per agent),
  u_nom and u for the last 10 s only (for the non-settler classification), into
  `results/amend39_settled_regen/<stage>/<cell>/`. Also `settled_seed{s}.json`.
- At every stage end: a sha256 manifest `ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.txt` of all traj/settled files. The manifest and every
  settled/metrics json are git-committed by `checkpoint` (push_lib.sh). The npz are not committed to git.
- The workstation pulls the archive while the pod is up (`logs/overnight/amend39_pull_archive.sh`, rsync over the pod's
  SSH) and verifies it against the manifest. A pod-side push is not used: the pod had no write access to the
  workstation in amend35.
- The pod volume is not deleted until the workstation copy has been verified against the manifest.
- The gate-only macro caches (≈13 GB each) are deleted after stage C; the five per-N caches are shared by A, B, D and
  kept until the end (the 150 GB quota incident).

## Compute (estimate from registered wall_seconds: 10–40 s per rollout on A100)

- 690 rollouts × ~25 s, 2 processes in parallel → about 2.5 h.
- Macro solves: 5 per-N caches + 4 extra gate caches. That is ≈ 9 × 8 min ≈ 1.2 h. A serial warm phase (seed 0 of every
  cell) runs before the parallel seeds, so a solve never runs next to a rollout (OOM history).
- Total about 4 h on one A100-80GB, plus archive transfer. Volume ≥ 200 GB.
